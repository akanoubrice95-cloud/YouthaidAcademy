    Personal data

    Privacy policy

    Last updated: 19 November 2026

    Youthaid Academy cares deeply about protecting your personal data, especially that of the young people we support. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

    Data collected

    Through the site’s forms (contact, membership, mentor, volunteer): name, email, city and any information you choose to share. Forms open your email app: data reaches us only through the email you send.

    Purposes

    • Answering your requests;
    • Processing memberships and applications (mentors, volunteers);
    • Organising support and matches;
    • Keeping you informed of our work, if you agreed.

    Legal basis

    Your consent and, for memberships, the performance of your relationship with the association.

    Recipients and retention

    Your data is only used by the Youthaid Academy team and is never sold. It is kept as long as needed to handle your request, then for at most 3 years after our last exchange.

    Minors

    For young people under 15, consent from a parent or legal guardian is required.

    Your rights

    You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object and port your data. To exercise them, write to us via the Contact page. You may also contact the CNIL (cnil.fr).

    Cookies

    See our cookie policy.
