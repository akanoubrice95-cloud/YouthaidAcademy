    About

    A foundation so every young person gets their chance

    Youthaid Academy is a social initiative dedicated to equal opportunities for young people from disadvantaged backgrounds.

    Why Youthaid Academy?

    Too many talented young people see their ambitions held back by a lack of network, information or support. Youthaid Academy was created to change that.

    We support young people facing difficulties towards academic, professional and entrepreneurial success by connecting them with mentors from many professional fields, and by offering them opportunities for training, jobs, internships, apprenticeships, student jobs and civic engagement in their local community and beyond.

    • Connect young people with committed mentors
    • Train through accessible online learning paths
    • Open access to academic and professional opportunities
    • Bring together a community of members, volunteers and partners
    • Measure our impact transparently
