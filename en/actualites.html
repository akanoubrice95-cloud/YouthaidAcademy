    Youthaid Academy launches its website

    We’re delighted to introduce the Youthaid Academy website. Here you’ll discover our mission, our programmes and all the ways to join the adventure: become a member, mentor or volunteer, partner with us or donate.

    This website is the first step towards a more complete platform: member area, mentor matching, online courses, opportunities and events will arrive step by step.

    Stay tuned, and feel free to get in touch!
