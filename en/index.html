    Welcome to Youthaid Academy

    Build your future, seize every opportunity.

    Youthaid Academy supports young people from disadvantaged backgrounds towards academic, professional and entrepreneurial success: committed mentors, training to build your skills and real opportunities.

      Youthaid Academy is a foundation that helps young people build their skills and connect with opportunities to shape their future and unlock their potential.

      Wherever you come from: mentoring, training, opportunities and civic engagement come together in one place, so every young person can find their own path.

      What we do

      Everything you need to move forward, in one place

      How it works

      Your journey in 4 steps

      I sign up

      I create my profile and share my goals, needs and interests.

      I get support

      I’m matched with a mentor based on my project, and the team approves every match.

      I learn

      I follow online learning paths and earn certificates.

      I seize my chance

      I apply to opportunities that suit me: internship, job, apprenticeship, scholarship…
