    Mentoring

    A mentor changes everything

    Mentoring is at the heart of Youthaid Academy: a volunteer professional supports a young person, listens, advises and opens up their network.

    Request a mentorBecome a mentor
    How it works

    Structured support

    Your request

    You describe your needs, your project and your availability.

    The match

    We suggest a mentor based on clear criteria: field, goals, language, location.

    Approval

    Every pair is approved by a team coordinator.

    Follow-up

    Regular meetings, a progress log and an end-of-programme review.

    For young people

    A caring outside view, practical advice, a network that opens up and someone who believes in you.

    For mentors

    Pass on knowledge, give meaning to your experience and make a real contribution to equal opportunities.

    A safe framework

    Approved matches, a commitment charter and supervision by our coordinators.

    Youthaidacademy

    Acting for equal opportunities: supporting young people towards academic, professional and entrepreneurial success.
